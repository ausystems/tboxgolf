About Thibodaux, Louisiana

Built for Thibodaux.

Three indoor golf simulator bays for a town that loves the game and deserves a place to play it all year.

Why T-Box

Golf weather, every day.

South Louisiana is a great place to love golf and a tough place to practice it. So we brought the course indoors.

  • 60Inches of rain a year, give or take
  • 90°FAnd up, for months every summer
  • 0Driving ranges in town
  • 3Indoor bays, rain or shine

A place to practice, all year.

Thibodaux has a strong junior golf program at Bayou Country Club and plenty of golfers who love the game. What it has not had is a driving range, or anywhere to play when the rain rolls in or the heat sets in.

T-Box changes that. Three simulator bays, open to everyone, with memberships for the regulars and room for parties and team events.

It is fully automated, so there is no front desk and no waiting. Book online, get your door code and let yourself in.

The tech

See every swing.

Each bay pairs launch monitor cameras with a big screen, so every shot shows up the moment you hit it.

  • Camera tracking

    Cameras read ball speed, launch, spin and carry on every swing.

  • Courses and range

    Play a full round, or work on your game at the range with instant numbers.

  • Fully automated

    Book online, get a door code and let yourself in. No front desk, no waiting.

  • Year round

    Heat, rain or a cold snap, the bays play the same every day.
