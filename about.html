Why we built it

Thibodaux has a strong junior program at Bayou Country Club, but there’s no driving range in town. Between the rain and the summer heat, a lot of practice days get lost.

So we built three simulator bays. Anyone can book one, and regulars can save with a membership.

There’s no front desk. Book online, get your door code and let yourself in.
