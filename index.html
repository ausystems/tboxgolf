How it works

Book. Unlock. Play.

T-Box is fully automated, so your bay is ready when you are. No front desk and no waiting on anyone.

  • Book online

    Pick a bay and a time from your phone. It takes about a minute.

  • Unlock with your code

    Your confirmation comes with a door code. Tap it in at the keypad and walk right in.

  • Play

    Pick a course or head to the range. The bay is yours until your time is up.
