How it works

There’s no front desk at T-Box. Everything runs from your phone.

  1. Book online

    Pick a bay and a time on your phone.

  2. Unlock with your code

    Your confirmation comes with a door code. Tap it in at the keypad and head to your bay.

  3. Play

    Pick a course or the range. The bay’s yours until your time runs out.
