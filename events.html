Questions

Before you book.

Have a question that is not here? Ask it in your inquiry.

Yes. T-Box is BYOB, and catering is available for events.

How many people can we bring?

Events work best for groups of 4 to 25.

Can we book an event online?

Events are booked through the inquiry form so we can plan the details with you. For a regular tee time, you can book online any time.

Do you offer leagues or lessons?

Not yet. Leagues and lessons are in the works for after we open.
