Questions

Good questions.

Still wondering about something? Send us a message.

Do I need a membership to play?

No. Anyone can book a bay at the walk-in rate: $40 an hour Monday to Thursday and $50 Friday to Sunday.

What happens when I use all my hours?

Keep playing. Members pay $20 for each extra hour.

How far ahead can I book?

Par members can book 3 days ahead, Birdie members 7 days and Eagle members 10 days.

How long can I play each day?

Members can book up to 2 hours a day.

When are members-only hours?

We plan to hold 5 to 9 AM and 8 to 11 PM for members. Final hours are set at launch.

How do I get into the building?

Every booking comes with a door code. Tap it in at the keypad and walk right in.

What is a founding membership?

Founding memberships are sold before we open, so you are part of T-Box from the very first swing.
