Before you join

Don’t see your question? Send us a message.

Do I need a membership to play?

No. Anyone can book a bay at the walk-in rate: $40 an hour Monday to Thursday and $50 Friday to Sunday.

What happens when I use up my hours?

Keep playing. Extra hours are $20 each for members.

How far ahead can I book?

Par members can book 3 days ahead, Birdie members 7 days and Eagle members 10 days.

How long can I play each day?

Members can book up to 2 hours a day.

When are members-only hours?

We’re planning on 5 to 9 AM and 8 to 11 PM. Final hours are set at launch.

How do I get in?

Your booking comes with a door code. Tap it in at the keypad by the door.

What’s a founding membership?

A membership you buy before we open. They’re only sold until opening day.
